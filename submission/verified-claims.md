## Required provenance labels

For public replay and offline simulation, retain these exact visible labels: `proof_class=synthetic_multi_persona`, `actor_type=simulated_persona`, `identity_source=synthetic_fixture`, `transport=fake_caspian`, `human_attested=false`, and `live_provider_verified=false`. Source: [submission proof design](../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-13-humanwire-submission-demo-proof-design.md#proof-classes).

## Citation rule

Submission packets may cite this ledger, but must preserve the linked source’s limitation and proof class. A claim with pending private or external proof stays pending even if a local test, screenshot, fixture, or fake transport exists.
