# HumanWire — professional 80-second narration

Important decisions fail when objection, evidence, and authority never meet in one accountable workflow. Here is HumanWire.

A strategy manager asks HumanWire to coordinate tomorrow's launch decision. It reads the objective, selects the minimum necessary stakeholder path, and begins through one channel-neutral Caspian gateway.

Maya and Nora acknowledge. Anika flags a rollback risk, so HumanWire pauses and opens a focused interview—not another meeting. Her answer becomes shareable evidence after confirmation. That evidence revises the proposal.

Now the order matters. Sofia approves the revised plan. Only then does Daniel provide availability. HumanWire packages the decision, participants, confirmed evidence, and agreed time into a meeting-ready result, ready for the responsible people to act.

Every saved event stays inspectable. Reviewers move backward and forward through the exact conversation, play the sequence, follow live progress, and export matching JSON and CSV records for review and audit.

Caspian is the configurable, consented-delivery boundary for approved channels. This public demonstration uses Standard agents and sends no external messages.

HumanWire turns one mandate into the right conversations, confirmed evidence, accountable approval, and a decision-ready meeting—without burying the team in another coordination thread.
